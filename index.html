⬇ Download for Android

v1.9 · Android 8.0+

Heads up: Chat-Later isn't on the Google Play Store yet, so Android will warn you about installing from outside the Store — that's expected, and safe to allow for this app.

How to install it

  1. Tap Download for Android above using your phone's browser.
  2. When the download finishes, tap the file (usually shown in a notification, or in your Downloads app).
  3. Android will say "Install blocked" or ask to allow this source — tap Settings, turn on "Allow from this source", then go back and tap Install.
  4. If you had an earlier version of Chat-Later installed, Android may ask you to uninstall it first — do that, then install this one.
  5. Once installed, open Chat-Later from your app drawer and create an account to start your free trial.
