Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This policy covers the Chat-Later Android app ("Chat-Later"), made by Titan Business Pros LLC ("we", "us"). It explains what information Chat-Later collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account info: when you sign up, we collect the email address and password you provide, handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Trial & subscription status: your account's trial start date and whether you've paid, stored in our database (Firebase Firestore) and tied to your account.
  • Payment details: if you subscribe, your payment is collected and processed directly by Stripe. We never receive or store your card number — we only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded.

Your voice and translations

Speech recognition, translation, and text-to-speech all run using your device's own Android system services and on-device translation models. Chat-Later's own servers do not receive, transmit, or store the words you speak or translate.

How we use what we collect

We use account and trial data only to run your account, enforce the free trial period, and unlock the app once you've paid. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that help run the app — Google Firebase (accounts and database) and Stripe (payments) — each governed by their own privacy policies. We don't sell or rent your information to anyone else.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account data as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the information below.

Children

Chat-Later is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
